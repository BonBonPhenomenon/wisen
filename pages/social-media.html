Key insights
Insight 01

Scope, not quality, explains the boutique price range

Within the boutique agency tier, NZ prices range from NZD$299 to NZD$4,000/mo. The difference reflects whether original short-form video and custom creative are included — not the quality of the agency. Both ends of the range can legitimately be described as boutique social media management.

Insight 02

NZ and AU freelancer rates are closely aligned

Experienced NZ freelancers place at NZD$80–$85/hr. AU equivalents average AUD$66/hr with active placements at AUD$65–$85/hr. After exchange rate adjustment, both markets sit within the same band — NZ is not materially cheaper than AU for skilled social media roles.

Insight 03

Social media is now a search and discovery channel

TikTok and Instagram are being used as search engines by 18–35-year-olds across both markets. This shifts the objective of organic social from reach and engagement to discoverability — strengthening the return on a professionally managed organic social presence.

Insight 04

Both markets are accelerating toward video

IAB AU recorded its strongest ever March quarter at AUD$4.9 billion in Q1 2026, with social video the fastest-growing sub-segment at +29.4% YoY. NZ video grew +27% in CY2025. Video-first content is now a scope-defining expectation at boutique level and above in both markets.

Insight 05

NZ/AU SMEs significantly underinvest in marketing

NZ and AU SMEs spend an average of 2.02% of revenue on marketing — against a Gartner global benchmark of 7.7%. Professional social media management represents a meaningful opportunity relative to current sector investment levels. (Vanguard 86 FY2026; 22-business sample — directional context only.)

About this report

This report covers organic social media management pricing across four active tiers: junior freelancer, experienced freelancer, boutique agency, and — in AU only — mid-sized agency. NZ mid-sized and large agency pricing is not published; those tiers are noted as proposal-only throughout. Paid social advertising management is covered separately in Report 04 — Paid Ads Benchmarks.

Confidence levels used throughout: Source-verified figures come from published pricing pages or active placement listings accessed during the research period. Estimated figures are derived from source-verified data using disclosed methodology — typically hourly rate × estimated retainer hours.

Market Context
AU market

Under-16 social media ban

Australia's ban on social media use by under-16s was enforced in December 2025. AU audiences on TikTok, Instagram, and Snapchat are now slightly older on average. This is relevant when assessing platform strategy and content direction as part of a social media management brief.

Both markets

AI is reshaping production workflows

AI tools have reduced time required for basic copywriting, image editing, and scheduling, softening demand for some junior and BAU content roles. Simultaneously, demand is strong for senior practitioners who can direct AI workflows, develop brand strategy, and produce video. Net effect: soft downward pressure on junior rates; senior specialist rates hold or grow.

Both markets

Social platforms as search engines

NZ and AU consumers aged 18–35 are increasingly using Instagram and TikTok for product discovery and local business research. This shifts the objective of organic social from reach and engagement to discoverability — changing the strategic value of a professionally managed social presence.
